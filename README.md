## About INHOST

**INHOST** is the hosting and domain-services brand operated by **PROCESS IN**, a software engineering and AI solutions company based in North Macedonia.

Our work connects digital infrastructure with the software and business systems that depend on it.

### Areas of service

- **Web hosting** — hosting solutions for websites and online projects.
- **Domain services** — domain registration and management.
- **Infrastructure** — server-related services and technical operations.
- **Business continuity** — infrastructure considerations for reliable online services.

For current service availability, packages, prices and terms, consult the [official INHOST website](https://inhost.mk).
